## Sources

- Official SOSP 2023 accepted-papers page: https://sosp2023.mpi-sws.org/accepted.html — parsed with `curl` + a local HTML stripper, yielding **43** titles (alphabetical; this is the enumeration order used in the "All papers" table below).
- Official SOSP 2023 program page: https://sosp2023.mpi-sws.org/program.html — parsed the same way. Sessions 1–11 contain 10 sessions of 4 papers plus one session of 3 = **43** talks, and the page carries exactly **43 distinct ACM DL DOIs** (`10.1145/3600006.36131xx`, plus `3613208` for Project Silica and `3624566` for Arboretum). Every DOI maps 1:1 onto a title from the accepted-papers page, so the two independent sources agree on 43 main-track papers with no extras and no omissions.
- DBLP (`dblp.org/db/conf/sosp/sosp2023.html`) and the ACM DL TOC (`dl.acm.org/doi/proceedings/10.1145/3600006`) were both fetched but are behind bot challenges (Anubis / Cloudflare), so the two MPI-SWS pages above are the authoritative cross-check pair. SRC (Student Research Competition) presentations and workshop items listed on the program page were excluded as non-main-track.
- Artifact/code links: SOSP 2023 Artifact Evaluation results page https://sysartifacts.github.io/sosp2023/results (34 functional / 33 reproduced / 25 available artifacts) — parsed to get DOI → artifact-URL pairs. Repo landing pages (README text only) were read for the borderline papers. For papers absent from the AE page, targeted web searches were used.

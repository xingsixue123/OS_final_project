## Sources

- Primary enumeration: the official USENIX ATC '23 program page, fetched raw with `curl` and parsed with a regex over `<a href="/conference/atc23/presentation/...">` anchors — https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc23/technical-sessions (3 conference days, Mon 2023-07-10 to Wed 2023-07-12). Parsing yielded 66 unique presentation links; one of them is the USENIX ATC '23 / OSDI '23 joint keynote ("Sky Computing"), which is not a main-track paper, leaving **65** papers.
- Cross-check of the count: the PC co-chairs' message, https://www.usenix.org/sites/default/files/atc23_message.pdf, states "…culminated in **65 accepted works** that will be presented at the conference" (from 353 submissions, including 22 deployed-systems and 19 short papers — all of which are main-track and are therefore counted here). This matches the parsed program exactly. DBLP was attempted as a third source but is behind an anti-bot challenge (Anubis) and could not be fetched.
- Abstracts and PDF links: each of the 65 individual `/conference/atc23/presentation/<slug>` pages was fetched and the `field-paper-description` block plus the `/system/files/atc23-*.pdf` link were extracted.
- Code: the USENIX ATC '23 artifact-evaluation results page https://sysartifacts.github.io/atc2023/results (41 artifacts with badges and repository URLs) was the main source, supplemented by GitHub search, author/lab pages (e.g. https://sra.uni-hannover.de/Publications/2023/melf-usenix-atc23/), and grepping the paper PDFs for repository URLs for papers absent from the AE list.

Scratch files (downloaded HTML/PDF, parsed lists) are under `scratch/`.

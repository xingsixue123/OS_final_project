Concerns - the process rewriter is a modified CRIU plus CRIT, and CRIU checkpoint/restore normally needs root or CAP_CHECKPOINT_RESTORE; unprivileged operation would have to go through a user namespace (bwrap is available), so feasibility of the very first checkpoint must be confirmed before committing to this paper |

## Sources

- Primary list: the official USENIX ATC '25 program, https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc25/technical-sessions , fetched with `curl` and parsed from raw HTML (not summarized). The page contains 102 nodes of class `node-paper`; two of them are non-papers (the USENIX Lifetime Achievement Flame Award item and the joint ATC '25 / OSDI '25 keynote by Emery Berger), leaving **100 main-track papers**. Each paper's own USENIX page was also fetched (100 pages) to extract the abstract, author list and the direct `/system/files/atc25-*.pdf` link, and every PDF was downloaded and converted to text to look for artifact URLs and evaluation hardware.
- Cross-check on the count: the ATC 2025 artifact-evaluation results page, https://sysartifacts.github.io/atc2025/results , states "Submissions : 46 (46% of accepted papers)". 46 / 0.46 = 100, which matches the 100 papers enumerated from the program. That page also gave the Available / Functional / Reproduced badges used below.
- DBLP (https://dblp.org/db/conf/usenix/usenix2025.html) was attempted as a second independent list but is currently behind an anti-bot interstitial and returned no data; the USENIX `atc25/proceedings` and `atc25/accepted-papers` URLs return 404, so the artifact-evaluation percentage above is the cross-check that was actually usable.
- Repository hunting: URLs extracted from the full text of every PDF (paper footnotes, availability statements and artifact appendices), plus the artifact-evaluation results page, author and lab GitHub/GitLab pages, project home pages (e.g. kben.sh for Koala), and GitHub repository search for the remaining systems.

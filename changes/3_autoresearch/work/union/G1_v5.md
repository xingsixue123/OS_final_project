## What this shows
- With default flags the v5 simulator and trainer are the frozen artifact (bit-identical results, series, histograms, counters; byte-identical models under pinned training).
- The v5 edits leave the alpha and class-table paths bit-identical to v4.
- The union path (`NewMLAP._union_accept`) is the single-model mechanism when the second clause cannot fire (kappa = 1e9, with the longer collected feature vector) and when both clauses are the same model: both reproduce the A+alpha run bit-for-bit. Union runs differ from single-model runs only through the second model's admissions.

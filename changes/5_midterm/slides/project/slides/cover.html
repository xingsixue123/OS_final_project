Ching-Hao Chiu · Jie Fu · Sixue Xing

University of Notre Dame

Disks are sized for the busiest 10 minutes

Mid-term presentation · October 2026

We are PeakBaleen. Baleen, from FAST ’24, decides what goes into a flash cache, but it optimizes average disk load, while disks are provisioned for the busiest 10 minutes. Our question from the proposal: with the same flash-write budget, can a peak-aware, Ising/QUBO-based selection lower that peak? Today: the baseline, the tasks we ran, and the design we settled on.

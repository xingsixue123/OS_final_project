These are the baselines we ran ourselves, all at the same flash write rate. First row: the paper’s Figure 9 average, for reference. As a reminder, each Region is one of Meta’s storage clusters, and s-numbers are 0.1% samples of its blocks. Then our reproduction on sample s0 of Region6 and Region7, and the held-out samples we use for testing on four Regions. CoinFlip admits randomly, RejectX rejects first-time items, Baleen is the ML policy. Baleen is clearly the strongest deployed baseline. The last column is Baleen’s own offline OPT, the oracle it is trained to imitate: on held-out sets Baleen online is 14 to 25% above it. That gap is our headroom, and the oracle itself still optimizes the average, not the peak.

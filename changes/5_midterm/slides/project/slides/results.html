Where we are. Offline, the min–max teacher lowers the peak by 13.7% on average and wins 37 of 39 instances. Deploying the full design, labels plus gate, failed its pre-registered test because one instance collapsed: its peak is a big sequential scan, and labels fitted to day 1 taught the model not to admit scans. The gate alone on Baleen’s own models helps: minus 3.2% on Region5, a cluster we had never run, in a pre-registered test, and large gains on Region4, which we only observed after the fact; it does not help on the low-load Region1. And the Ising solver is not the reason for the offline gain: LP and CP-SAT do as well.

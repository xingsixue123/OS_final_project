Left is Baleen: it sorts episodes by disk time saved per byte, labels the prefix that fits the write budget, trains a GBM, and online admits when the score beats theta. Right is PeakBaleen. Orange is the Ising/QUBO core: the load left in each window, a smooth max over windows plus a quadratic write-budget penalty, solved by parallel tempering with 16 replicas from an LP warm start. Its solution becomes the labels. Blue is the rest of what is new: the GBM also sees a causal load signal, and online the bar is scaled by the load of the last ten minutes, lower when busy. Gray is unchanged from Baleen, including tuning theta to the same write rate.
